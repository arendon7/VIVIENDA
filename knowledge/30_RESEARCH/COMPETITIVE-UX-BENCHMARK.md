# VIVIENDA — Competitive UX Benchmark V0.1

Research date: 2026-08-25
Status: Current web benchmark; revalidate before major product decisions because financial pages/rates change.

## Benchmark method

This benchmark evaluates current products by user task, not by visual taste alone.

Dimensions:

1. clarity of entry promise;
2. time to first value;
3. identity/contact gate;
4. data requested;
5. result usefulness;
6. transparency/provenance;
7. transition from result to execution;
8. trust/disclosure;
9. long-term relationship potential;
10. lessons for VIVIENDA.

## Executive synthesis

### Strongest patterns to borrow

- KrediHub: immediate calculator-led acquisition and current-rate comparison.
- La Haus: financing simulation embedded in the property decision context.
- Habi/Habicredit: strong “do not go bank by bank” simplification and rapid pre-approval promise.
- Bancolombia: explicit DIY/free-modification transparency.
- FNA: detailed product conditions and dated/current rate information.
- NerdWallet: visible rate date, advertiser/editorial disclosure, APR + fees + payment comparison.
- Better: clear online journey, assumption disclaimers, account/process transparency and “clarity/control” positioning.
- Rocket Mortgage: goal-based calculator recommendation and calculator library.
- Habito: education content that naturally routes to a mortgage expert/remortgage action.

### Largest opportunity for VIVIENDA

No reviewed product combines all of the following as its primary experience:

- decision comparison beyond lender cards;
- existing-loan longitudinal Mortgage Twin;
- transparent decomposition of projected benefit;
- DIY vs assisted route transparency;
- Colombian legal-rights intelligence;
- legal escalation when a financial workflow becomes a dispute;
- one coherent relationship before and after origination.

That combination should remain the strategic target.

## 1. KrediHub — Colombian comparator/origination benchmark

Sources reviewed:

- https://kredihub.co/
- https://kredihub.co/simulador-hipotecario
- https://kredihub.co/comparar-bancos
- https://kredihub.co/base-conocimiento

### What it does well

#### Utility-first framing

The dedicated mortgage simulator states that it compares current/reference conditions across multiple Colombian banks and emphasizes speed and no-cost access.

This validates a core VIVIENDA principle: a calculator can be the acquisition surface, not merely a feature hidden inside an app.

#### Strong market simplification

The problem narrative is direct:

- going bank by bank is slow;
- rates differ;
- the user needs comparison.

This matches real user friction better than institutional product descriptions.

#### Broad product surface

KrediHub has expanded beyond mortgage into leasing, portfolio purchase, vehicle, free-investment and insurance products. This demonstrates that successful comparison platforms tend toward marketplace breadth.

For VIVIENDA this is both validation and a warning: horizontal credit breadth is not our desired moat.

#### Operational knowledge base

Its public knowledge base includes bank-specific rate/product tables and conditions, demonstrating the value of a structured operational layer behind the consumer experience.

VIVIENDA should build a more formally versioned equivalent for verified bank procedures and rate provenance.

### Friction / inconsistencies observed

The dedicated simulator page says “without registration,” while current homepage interaction text indicates a mobile number is requested to view certain results. This inconsistency can undermine trust even if commercially effective.

Lesson:

**Do not make “no registration” claims unless first meaningful result truly remains ungated.**

### Strategic lesson

Do not compete by merely adding more banks. Compete by helping users decide among actions and explaining the economics/legal implications behind those actions.

## 2. Habi / Habicredit — assisted financing benchmark

Sources reviewed:

- https://habi.co/
- https://centro-ayuda.habi.co/hc/es-419/articles/23309292753307--Qu%C3%A9-es-Habicredit
- https://centro-ayuda.habi.co/hc/es-419/articles/23309381338395-Solicitar-un-cr%C3%A9dito

### What it does well

#### Outcome-focused language

Habicredit frames itself around obtaining real-estate financing without making requests bank-by-bank.

#### Speed/low-friction promise

Its support materials emphasize rapid preapproval/simulation and WhatsApp as a familiar channel.

#### Ecosystem adjacency

Because Habi already participates in property transactions, credit is naturally adjacent to an existing user journey.

### Opportunity for VIVIENDA

VIVIENDA is not attached to a property inventory, so it must earn relevance through superior decision intelligence rather than transaction ownership.

We should avoid copying a “dream home” sales tone as the primary voice; our strongest differentiation is independence and rigorous explanation.

## 3. La Haus — contextual financing inside property discovery

Sources reviewed:

- current property pages at https://www.lahaus.com/

### What it does well

Property pages embed a credit simulation directly beside the property context, including property value, financed amount, term, monthly payment and required income.

This is a powerful contextual UX pattern:

**do not make users leave the decision they are making in order to understand financing.**

### Opportunity for VIVIENDA

VIVIENDA should apply the same principle without becoming a listings portal.

Examples:

- after affordability result → compare financing;
- after loan-health result → show relevant prepayment/transfer actions;
- after an educational article → launch the relevant calculator with context preserved.

## 4. Bancolombia — DIY transparency benchmark

Sources reviewed:

- https://www.bancolombia.com/centro-de-ayuda/preguntas-frecuentes/obtener-asesoria-gratuita-modificaciones-credito-vivienda
- https://www.bancolombia.com/centro-de-ayuda/preguntas-frecuentes/cuota-credito-hipotecario-abono

### What it does well

Bancolombia explicitly states that users can request several housing-credit modifications directly and without cost or intermediaries, and it clearly distinguishes extra-payment application to term reduction vs installment reduction.

### Strategic lesson

This should become a VIVIENDA trust differentiator:

> If the user can safely do something directly and free, say so.

Our paid value must be analysis, comparison, execution complexity, monitoring or professional intervention — not hiding a free bank channel.

### UX opportunity

Every opportunity card can include:

- “Puedes hacerlo directamente”
- “Qué pedir exactamente”
- “Te acompañamos si prefieres”

This creates agency and can increase trust in later paid pathways.

## 5. Fondo Nacional del Ahorro — product-detail and freshness benchmark

Sources reviewed:

- https://www.fna.gov.co/transparencia/informacion-entidad/tasas
- https://www.fna.gov.co/personas/vivienda/credito-hipotecario/compra-de-cartera-hipotecaria

### What it does well

Current pages expose:

- specific rate tables;
- currency modality;
- income bands;
- product limitations;
- financing percentage/term details;
- explicit update dates on product pages.

For portfolio purchase, FNA also explains relevant constraints such as percentage of outstanding balance/commercial value and product exclusions.

### Strategic lesson

VIVIENDA needs a visible freshness system for market data:

- source;
- effective date;
- last verified date;
- conditions/segment;
- whether rate is public reference or personalized offer.

Do not simply display “Desde X%” without context.

## 6. NerdWallet — comparison transparency benchmark

Source reviewed:

- https://www.nerdwallet.com/mortgages/mortgage-rates

### What it does well

The current rate comparison experience shows:

- rate freshness/date;
- APR;
- interest rate;
- estimated monthly payment;
- total fees;
- customization inputs;
- advertiser disclosure/editorial integrity cues;
- expandable product detail.

### Strategic lesson

A ranking should not be a black box.

VIVIENDA comparison rows/cards should expose multiple dimensions, such as:

- monthly payment;
- total estimated cost;
- rate/APR-equivalent where locally meaningful;
- fees/costs;
- modality/risk;
- eligibility/compatibility confidence;
- source/freshness;
- reason for ranking.

If VIVIENDA receives compensation from a partner, disclose the commercial relationship without allowing it to silently determine ranking.

## 7. Better Mortgage — digital journey and trust benchmark

Sources reviewed:

- https://better.com/mortgage
- https://better.com/content/refinance-calculator
- https://better.com/loan-comparison-calculator
- https://better.com/b/rates

### What it does well

#### Clear category simplification

Better explicitly positions against a confusing traditional mortgage process using “clarity and control.”

#### Strong process visibility

It emphasizes an online account where users can see where they are in the process.

#### Disclosures near calculations

Its refinance/comparison content states that calculator outputs are illustrative and explains assumptions such as reinvestment of savings in one model.

This is particularly important: a sophisticated savings number is only trustworthy when methodology is visible.

### Strategic lesson

VIVIENDA should treat methodology disclosure as product design, not footer legal text.

For every major simulation:

- plain-language result;
- “Cómo calculamos esto”;
- editable assumptions;
- limitations;
- source/date.

## 8. Rocket Mortgage — goal-based tool discovery benchmark

Sources reviewed:

- https://www.rocketmortgage.com/calculators
- https://www.rocketmortgage.com/refinance-calculators
- https://www.rocketmortgage.com/calculators/refinance-calculator

### What it does well

Rocket maintains a calculator family but does not require the user to understand which calculator they need; it can ask goal/profile questions and recommend the best fit.

Its tools include affordability, mortgage payment, down payment, refinance, equity and rent-vs-buy themes.

### Strategic lesson

As VIVIENDA adds tools, do not expose a “calculator catalog” as the only IA.

Use intent routing:

- “Quiero pagar menos”
- “Quiero terminar antes”
- “Quiero saber cuánto puedo comprar”
- “Quiero comparar si me conviene cambiar de entidad”

Then route to the correct engine.

## 9. Habito — content-to-expert benchmark

Sources reviewed:

- https://www.habito.com/remortgaging/online-remortgage
- https://www.habito.com/remortgaging/what-is-a-remortgage

### What it does well

Habito publishes detailed remortgage education and keeps a contextual expert CTA in the learning journey.

The tone is approachable rather than institutional.

### Strategic lesson

VIVIENDA educational content should have a relevant next utility, not a universal “contact us.”

Example:

- Law 546 article → restructuring screener;
- prepayment article → prepayment tool;
- UVR article → scenario explainer;
- portfolio transfer article → comparison.

# Cross-competitor pattern matrix

| Pattern | Market evidence | VIVIENDA decision |
|---|---|---|
| Calculator-led acquisition | KrediHub, Rocket, Better | Adopt strongly |
| Phone gate before useful detail | Present in parts of local funnels | Avoid before first meaningful value |
| Bank-by-bank simplification | KrediHub, Habi | Adopt, but expand to decision comparison |
| Property-context simulation | La Haus | Adopt context principle, not listings business |
| DIY transparency | Bancolombia | Make it a trust differentiator |
| Fresh rates/source dates | FNA, NerdWallet, Better | Required provenance pattern |
| Fees beyond rate | NerdWallet | Adopt in comparisons |
| Educational content → action | Habito, Rocket | Adopt |

| Broad marketplace expansion | KrediHub | Resist until core vertical is proven |
| “Dream home” emotional framing | Habi/FNA/industry | Use selectively; do not replace financial clarity |

# Product implications

## Implication 1 — first value must truly be free

The strongest differentiation opportunity is to actually deliver a useful first result without phone, cédula or document upload.

## Implication 2 — comparison needs ranking provenance

If VIVIENDA says “mejor para ti,” users must be able to see why.

Candidate explanation:

> “Esta opción aparece primero porque, bajo tus supuestos, combina menor costo total, una cuota dentro de tu rango y condiciones compatibles con tu objetivo de plazo.”

## Implication 3 — display fresh data as a first-class UI object

Create a reusable source/freshness component:

- “Tasa pública de referencia”
- “Actualizada 21 ago 2026”
- source entity
- conditions link

## Implication 4 — execution status is a future retention surface

When a user chooses assistance, provide a visible tracker rather than migrating the entire relationship to WhatsApp.

WhatsApp may remain a channel, but the canonical status should live in VIVIENDA.

## Implication 5 — calculator family should be intent-driven

A “Tools” page can exist for SEO, but primary UX should ask the user's decision.

## Implication 6 — trust should be explicit about business model

If comparison/origination is free because a financial institution pays VIVIENDA, explain it. If that compensation can create conflicts, disclose ranking policy.

## Implication 7 — savings decomposition is our highest-value unexplored pattern

None of the reviewed experiences foregrounds a decomposition such as:

- benefit from user's additional principal;
- benefit from lower rate;
- benefit from lower insurance/fees;
- benefit from corrected error;
- VIVIENDA fees/costs;
- net benefit.

VIVIENDA should prototype this as a signature component.

# Visual implications

Do not infer a single visual style from this benchmark. Most competitors use familiar finance/real-estate patterns because they optimize trust and speed.

VIVIENDA can be more distinctive, but the distinctive layer should live in:

- typography;
- data visualization;
- transitions between decisions;
- proprietary result components;
- strong information hierarchy;
- a coherent Path/Horizon motif;
- calm, human imagery where appropriate.

Avoid novelty that makes calculations harder to audit.
