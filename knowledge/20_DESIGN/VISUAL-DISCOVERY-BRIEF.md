# VIVIENDA — Visual Discovery Brief V0.1

Date: 2026-08-25
Status: Discovery brief. This document explicitly does **not** freeze the visual system.

## Purpose

Find a visual language that makes VIVIENDA feel unusually trustworthy, usable and memorable in Colombian housing finance without becoming visually interchangeable with:

- a bank;
- a law firm;
- a fintech/crypto startup;
- a real-estate portal;
- a generic AI-generated SaaS landing.

The visual system must work across both **Persuade** surfaces and dense **Operate** surfaces.

## Brand/product tension to solve

VIVIENDA sits between emotional and analytical territory.

### Housing is emotional

Users associate a home with:

- security;
- independence;
- family;
- progress;
- uncertainty;
- long-term commitment.

### Credit is analytical

Users need:

- exact numbers;
- tradeoffs;
- dates;
- rates;
- assumptions;
- risk;
- documentation.

### Legal protection is high-trust

Users need:

- competence;
- precision;
- discretion;
- realistic expectations;
- control.

The design must hold all three without becoming sentimental, cold or intimidating.

## Desired perception

After 30 seconds, users should ideally describe VIVIENDA as:

- claro;
- serio;
- moderno;
- útil;
- tranquilo;
- diferente a un banco;
- no parece una estafa;
- siento que puedo entender mi decisión.

Avoid target perceptions such as:

- lujoso;
- exclusivo;
- disruptivo;
- agresivo;
- futurista;

unless research later supports them.

## Working visual principles

### 1. Numbers deserve visual authority

Financial metrics should be beautiful through typography, hierarchy and context — not through oversized vanity numerals without meaning.

### 2. Warm precision

Use warmth through material, typography, imagery and subtle tonal choices while keeping financial controls exact and predictable.

### 3. One accent, many semantic states

Brand accent should be controlled. Semantic success/warning/error/info colors should not compete with brand color.

### 4. Calm surfaces

Avoid excessive shadows, floating glass panels and nested cards. Use grouping through spacing, rules, background planes and typography.

### 5. Data is part of the identity

Charts, scenario comparisons, amortization curves, payment composition and timeline/path diagrams should become signature visual material rather than generic decoration.

### 6. Trust has a shape

Source/freshness, methodology, assumptions and “who decides this?” should have reusable, elegant visual patterns.

## Signature concept to explore — Path / Horizon

### Concept

A home-finance decision is not a single transaction; it is a path through time.

A subtle line/path motif could represent:

- remaining loan term;
- path to ownership;
- scenario divergence;
- financial progress;
- timeline/status;
- a route from uncertainty to decision.

### Why it fits

It supports product information architecture rather than adding arbitrary decoration.

### Risks

- becoming a literal road illustration;
- overusing line animations;
- looking like a logistics/travel brand;
- visually implying a guaranteed future trajectory.

The motif must remain abstract and data-compatible.

## Alternative concept families to explore

The discovery round should test at least three distinct directions.

### Direction A — Warm Instrument

Concept:

A precision instrument made approachable.

Characteristics:

- warm off-white/stone foundations;
- dark high-contrast text;
- one confident non-neon accent;
- excellent numeric typography;
- restrained lines/grids;
- soft photography of real homes/people used sparingly;
- motion low/moderate.

Strength:

Trust + consumer warmth.

Risk:

Could become too generic premium-fintech if not given a strong motif/type system.

### Direction B — Editorial Financial Guide

Concept:

A highly readable modern guide with a strong point of view.

Characteristics:

- editorial rhythm;
- striking but disciplined typography;
- strong information hierarchy;
- diagrams integrated with narrative;
- more asymmetric marketing layouts;
- product UI becomes calmer/denser.

Strength:

Can make education and trust feel distinctive.

Risk:

Over-editorial styling may hurt forms/dashboard consistency.

### Direction C — Path / Decision Map

Concept:

The product visualizes the consequences of choices over time.

Characteristics:

- path/branch geometry;
- scenario curves;
- interactive decision visualizations;
- progression motifs;
- moderate motion on marketing and scenario comparison;
- restrained product surfaces.

Strength:

Most directly tied to product moat.

Risk:

Requires exceptional design engineering to avoid gimmickry.

## Typography exploration

Do not default to Inter solely because it is safe.

Criteria:

- excellent Spanish accents/numerals;
- highly readable body text;
- tabular/financial numeric behavior where needed;
- credible at both 14–16px product text and expressive marketing scale;
- performant web delivery;
- broad weight range;
- distinctive without fashion-driven fragility.

Consider testing:

- modern grotesk/humanist sans families;
- potentially a secondary editorial face only if justified by the chosen direction;
- monospaced/tabular numerals for selected financial contexts, not as a general developer aesthetic.

Final font selection must be license/performance compatible.

## Color exploration

### Avoid defaults

- fintech electric blue as automatic choice;
- AI purple gradient;
- law-firm navy/gold;
- real-estate green without concept;
- all-black “luxury” treatment.

### Test roles, not colors first

Need tokens for:

- canvas;
- raised/subtle surfaces;
- primary text;
- secondary text;
- rule/border;
- brand accent;
- interactive accent;
- positive;
- caution;
- negative;
- info;
- chart series;
- selected/comparison highlight.

AA contrast must be achievable without muddy colors.

## Imagery policy

Prefer, in order:

1. product-generated data visualizations;
2. purposeful diagrams;
3. authentic Colombian/home-life photography where emotional context helps;
4. architecture/property detail imagery when relevant.

Avoid:

- stock couples holding keys;
- fake banker handshakes;
- generic house icon illustrations;
- laptop mockups filling empty hero space;
- generated financial charts that do not represent real data.

## Iconography

Use one coherent family.

Icons should support scanning, not decorate every heading.

Avoid house icons as universal category markers; housing identity should emerge from system language, not repeated roof glyphs.

## Motion hypothesis

### Marketing

Moderate and orchestrated.

Potential signature interaction:

A scenario/path visualization that demonstrates how a housing decision changes over time.

### Calculators

Motion only for:

- input-to-result continuity;
- scenario switching;
- reveal of explanation;
- progress/confirmation.

### Dashboard

Low-motion, state-oriented.

All critical information remains understandable with reduced motion enabled.

## Signature components to prototype visually

Before full design system, prototype these because they embody differentiation:

### 1. Decision Result

Shows:

- recommendation/action;
- confidence/type;
- primary economic effect;
- key tradeoff;
- next action.

### 2. Benefit Breakdown

Separates:

- user additional principal;
- rate/financial change;
- fees/insurance;
- corrected error;
- service costs;
- net projected benefit.

### 3. Source + Freshness

Shows:

- source;
- effective date;
- last verified;
- reference vs personalized status.

### 4. Scenario Path

Visual comparison across time, with accessible numeric table/summary.

### 5. DIY / Assisted Choice

Two legitimate paths without visually punishing the free option.

### 6. Mortgage Twin Snapshot

Current balance/payment/term/modality, provenance, last updated and one prioritized opportunity.

These components should become more visually memorable than generic marketing cards.

## Density strategy

### Marketing pages

Density: low/moderate.

Use space to prioritize ideas, but avoid excessively sparse “premium” pages that force scrolling for basic information.

### Calculators

Density: moderate.

Inputs and results should coexist on desktop where comprehension improves.

### Dashboard

Density: moderate/high with progressive detail.

Avoid presenting every metric at once; prioritize current state and decisions.

## Responsive strategy

The visual world must be designed mobile-first for acquisition.

Prototype at minimum:

- 390px mobile;
- 768px tablet boundary;
- 1280/1440 desktop.

Do not simply stack desktop cards vertically.

For mobile scenario comparison:

- show recommendation summary;
- allow clear scenario switching;
- preserve comparison context;
- provide accessible details on demand.

## Trust micro-patterns to design

- “Estimación” badge with explanation;
- “Oferta real” badge only when sourced from entity;
- “Revisión jurídica recomendada” state;
- “Puedes hacerlo directamente con tu banco” state;
- “Cómo calculamos esto” disclosure;
- data-source/freshness line;
- privacy purpose before upload;
- correction affordance after OCR.

These are brand components, not compliance leftovers.

## Visual anti-pattern checklist

Reject if direction depends on:

- gradient blob hero;
- glass card stacks;
- arbitrary bento because it is fashionable;
- meaningless 3D coins/houses;
- excessive rounded pills;
- all content inside bordered cards;
- animation on every scroll section;
- giant number with no denominator/source;
- low-contrast gray financial details;
- playful motion during legal/payment distress flows.

## Discovery evaluation score

Score each visual direction 1–5 on:

- trust;
- distinction;
- readability;
- financial-data fit;
- legal-content fit;
- marketing conversion potential;
- mobile fit;
- accessibility;
- engineering maintainability;
- ability to scale into authenticated product.

Do not select the prettiest landing if it fails product UI.

## Output of visual discovery

Before implementation, deliver:

1. mood/reference board with rationale;
2. 3 direction territories;
3. representative home hero/section;
4. calculator result;
5. Mortgage Twin snapshot;
6. mobile version;
7. direction scorecard;
8. chosen direction rationale;
9. initial `DESIGN.md` with tokens/rules.

Only then freeze a visual language.
