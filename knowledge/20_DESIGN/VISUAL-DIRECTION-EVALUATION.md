# VIVIENDA — Visual Direction Evaluation V0.1

## Goal
Select a visual direction for VIVIENDA based on comprehension, trust, mobile behavior, conversion, financial legibility, differentiation and scalability rather than taste alone.

The same borrower vertical slice was materialized in Figma across three territories:

- A — Warm Instrument
- B — Editorial Financial Guide
- C — Path / Decision Map

Screens compared:
- R1 Quick Check result
- S2 scenario
- S3 Benefit Breakdown
- M1 Mortgage Twin

## Territory A — Warm Instrument

### Strengths
- approachable without feeling childish;
- lowers emotional temperature around debt;
- cards and soft surfaces work naturally for onboarding and upload;
- supports confidence-building copy and permission requests;
- likely strong for mass-market Colombian consumer adoption.

### Weaknesses
- by itself can resemble polished fintech wellness;
- weaker ownable visual metaphor;
- card-heavy execution can become generic;
- comparison between scenarios is less structurally obvious.

## Territory B — Editorial Financial Guide

### Strengths
- strongest methodological seriousness;
- naturally supports source, freshness, assumptions and footnotes;
- excellent for rates, comparisons, legal/financial explanations;
- less likely to overstate an estimate;
- communicates independence and intelligence.

### Weaknesses
- risks feeling like an article or research report rather than a product;
- can become text-heavy on mobile;
- weaker interaction affordance;
- less emotional warmth and momentum;
- difficult to make memorable without an additional visual grammar.

## Territory C — Path / Decision Map

### Strengths
- creates a direct visual model of the product thesis: decisions change financial trajectories;
- scenario comparison becomes spatial rather than merely numeric;
- can unify progress, amortization, timeline, opportunity ranking and Mortgage Twin;
- differentiates VIVIENDA from rate-comparison tables and legal-service landing pages;
- highly compatible with motion used to explain cause and effect;
- provides a reusable brand asset without relying on house imagery.

### Weaknesses
- pure execution can feel cold or technical;
- path diagrams can become visually noisy when many choices exist;
- requires strict information hierarchy on mobile;
- metaphor must never imply guaranteed future outcomes.

# Decision

Select **Path / Decision Map as the structural visual grammar**, but not as a pure stylistic territory.

The canonical direction is a hybrid:

> **Path structure + Warm Instrument emotional tone + Editorial Financial Guide information discipline.**

Internal shorthand: **Warm Path**.

## What comes from Path / Decision Map
- trajectory as core metaphor;
- current state → decision → projected state;
- decision nodes;
- branching alternatives;
- progress through the life of the mortgage;
- scenario overlays;
- visual relation between user action and economic consequence.

## What comes from Warm Instrument
- warm neutral surfaces;
- calm pacing;
- rounded but restrained containers;
- low-threat upload and permission UX;
- human-language microcopy;
- generous touch areas and mobile comfort.

## What comes from Editorial Financial Guide
- source and freshness are visible;
- explicit labels for estimate / simulation / verified data / offer / approval;
- restrained typographic hierarchy;
- methodology access;
- no promotional number without context;
- tables only when comparison benefits from them.

## Reject
- blue-gradient fintech aesthetic;
- generic hero imagery of happy homeowners;
- excessive house/roof/key icons;
- faux dashboards full of vanity metrics;
- green = guaranteed savings;
- gamified credit scoring;
- decorative motion detached from financial causality;
- giant savings figures without Benefit Breakdown.

## Validation still required
This decision is strong enough to derive design tokens and a vertical slice, but visual detail remains subject to:
- mobile review;
- contrast/accessibility;
- comprehension usability testing;
- implementation constraints;
- conversion evidence after launch.
