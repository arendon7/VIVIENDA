# VIVIENDA — Product Marketing Context

Status: provisional foundation context. Update as customer research and traffic data arrive.

## Product

VIVIENDA is a Colombian platform for better financial and legal decisions around housing.

It helps users calculate, compare, understand, optimize and protect the financing of their home. It is designed to create value before asking the user to buy a service.

## Positioning hypothesis

**Category:** housing-finance decision and execution platform.

**Core promise:** help people understand what they can improve, compare realistic alternatives and execute the right next step around their home financing.

**Not positioned as:** a law-firm landing page, a lender, a property marketplace, a magical debt-reduction service or a hidden “Ley 546” trick.

## Strategic message

Working conceptual line:

> Your co-pilot for the financial and legal decisions around your home.

Possible simpler expression:

> Calculate. Compare. Optimize. Protect.

These are working hypotheses, not final brand copy.

## Primary audiences

### A. Existing housing-loan borrower

Situation:

- already has mortgage/housing credit or leasing;
- sees a large monthly payment and long remaining term;
- does not fully understand amortization, UVR/COP, insurance or alternatives;
- may have heard about prepayment, portfolio transfer or Law 546 through social media;
- wants a concrete answer, not a legal lecture.

Primary questions:

- Am I paying this the best way?
- Can I finish sooner?
- Can I pay less overall?
- Should I move the loan?
- Is the bank applying my payments correctly?

Desired first value:

A quantified, understandable view of the current loan and relevant scenarios.

### B. Prospective home buyer

Situation:

- considering buying within months/years;
- uncertain about sustainable price range;
- confused by rate, down payment, COP/UVR, leasing, FNA, subsidies and total ownership cost.

Primary questions:

- How much home can I realistically afford?
- Is buying now reasonable?
- What should I improve first?
- Which financing structure fits me?

Desired first value:

A sustainable target range and clear next actions.

### C. Borrower under financial pressure

Situation:

- payment has become difficult;
- income or household circumstances changed;
- may already be in collections or facing a lender dispute.

Primary questions:

- What can I do before this gets worse?
- Do I qualify for restructuring or another route?
- What should I document?
- When do I need a lawyer?

Desired first value:

Correct classification, urgency and legitimate next step without panic marketing.

## User anxieties

- “They only want my phone number to sell me something.”
- “This sounds too good to be true.”
- “I do not understand how they calculated the savings.”
- “Will this affect my credit score?”
- “Are they a bank?”
- “Is this really free?”
- “Why do they need my extract?”
- “Could I do this myself with my bank?”
- “What happens with my personal financial data?”
- “Is this a real legal right or just marketing?”

The product should proactively answer these objections through design, provenance and progressive disclosure.

## Differentiation hypotheses

### 1. Explain where value comes from

Separate benefit created by:

- additional money paid by the user;
- reduced rate;
- lower insurance/fees;
- corrected error;
- negotiation/execution;
- time/value assumptions.

### 2. Decisions, not just bank cards

Compare actions such as:

- do nothing;
- prepay;
- shorten term;
- reduce installment;
- wait and save;
- transfer portfolio;
- negotiate;
- restructure;
- obtain legal review.

### 3. Self-service honesty

If a user can safely execute a simple action directly and for free with the bank, say so. Paid help should correspond to analysis, execution or complexity that creates real value.

### 4. Financial + legal continuity

Financial decision support and legal protection can coexist in one user journey while maintaining a clear professional/commercial boundary.

### 5. Longitudinal Mortgage Twin

Instead of losing the customer after one transaction, maintain a living model of the loan and detect future opportunities/events.

## Competitor frame

The market contains:

- mortgage/credit comparators and brokers;
- real-estate portals;
- creators and advisory networks marketing Law 546/prepayment strategies;
- banks' own free tools and modification channels;
- law firms handling disputes or insolvency.

VIVIENDA should not attempt to beat every category at its own game. It should own the layer between **understanding a housing decision and executing the right next action**.

## Offer architecture hypothesis

### Free value

- affordability;
- payment simulation;
- buy vs rent;
- COP vs UVR education/scenarios;
- real cost of owning;
- prepayment impact;
- basic readiness/loan-health result.

### Higher-trust free value

After account/profile/document connection:

- saved scenarios;
- personalized profile;
- Mortgage Twin;
- personalized opportunities;
- monitoring/alerts.

### Paid/monetized value

Where validated:

- financial-product/application execution;
- portfolio transfer;
- advanced optimization;
- legal transaction review;
- bank complaints/negotiation;
- legal representation.

## Conversion principles

1. First meaningful result before phone number whenever possible.
2. Account creation after demonstrated value, not as a reflex gate.
3. Explain why a sensitive field/document is required before requesting it.
4. One dominant CTA per decision state.
5. Give users a self-service path and an assisted path when both are legitimate.
6. Do not use guarantees or fabricated urgency.
7. Show assumptions and provenance for material numbers.
8. Use high-intent contextual conversion rather than generic “talk to sales” everywhere.

## Funnel hypotheses

### Existing borrower

Social/search/content → prepayment/loan-health tool → quantified result → optional extract → precise Mortgage Twin → recommended action → self-service or assisted execution.

### Prospective buyer

Search/social → affordability/buy-vs-rent tool → result → Home Profile → personalized financing comparison → application/partner path → ongoing account.

### Legal problem

Search/content/dashboard alert → issue classifier → evidence checklist → preliminary route → lawyer review/engagement.

## Voice

### We should sound

- clear;
- calm;
- precise;
- useful;
- adult-to-adult;
- transparent about uncertainty;
- confident when law/math is verified.

### Avoid

- bank bureaucracy;
- aggressive debt-relief language;
- miracle claims;
- legalese in primary UX;
- infantilizing financial education;
- crypto/fintech hype;
- false scarcity.

## Evidence policy

Never claim:

- approval probability without a validated model and adequate explanation;
- guaranteed savings;
- guaranteed legal outcome;
- “average savings” without valid underlying dataset and methodology;
- partnerships not contractually active;
- market rates without date/source;
- customer counts/testimonials/results not evidenced.
